Who are your friends?

We have many groupchats and many friend groups, but we somehow always stick to the same friend group and sometimes feel uncomfortable to meet new people.

Let this chat be a place to open up and speak with your Muslim brothers who live in the same city as you and perhaps have the same struggles you have. Many lives are changed through brotherhood and we are ordered to strengthen our brotherhood.

The Prohpet Muhammad ﷺ said:

وَكُونُوا عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِخْوَانًا

Be brothers (as Allah has ordered you!) [Bukhari: 6064]

We know it's very hard to live in a land of disbelief, but good brotherhood is a tool Allah has given us to use and promised great through it.

Group motive:
To communicate with your Muslim brothers in the same city as you to make it easier for you to practice your religion.
