The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most consistent ones

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

أَحَبُّ الأَعْمَالِ إِلَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى أَدْوَمُهَا وَإِنْ قَلَّ

The acts most pleasing to Allah are those which are done continuously, even if they are small.
