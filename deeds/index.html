Khair Deeds

The point of knowledge is to act upon it

Knowledge without action results in bad character in the Dunya (worldly life) and severe punishment in the akhirah (afterlife).

Group Motive:

  • To share 1 good deed every once in a while, that you can easily do, that will aid you to Jannah and save you from Jahannam.

    • May Allah bless you all and grant you righteous actions, for knowledge without action is from the worst of positions.
